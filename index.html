How CostPilot Works

Transparent software estimation grounded in Bangalore's modern technology ecosystem.

  1. Understand Scope: We map your target product archetype, pages, forms, and custom workflows.
  2. Component Breakdown: We partition work into UI/UX, Frontend, Backend/API, Database, Integrations, and QA.
  3. Apply Market Benchmarks: Calculations utilize validated Bangalore engineering rates (low, typical, high).
  4. Recurring Infrastructure: Sizing includes compute, data storage, CDN edge delivery, and third-party APIs.
  5. Maintenance Estimation: We isolate ongoing patching and upgrades from hosting to illustrate 1, 3, and 5-year TCO.
Important Note: A market estimate is an indicative baseline for budgeting. It is not an unconditional contractor quote.
